## 2026-06-06 - CLI Visual Hierarchy and Feedback
**Learning:** For terminal-based developer tools, semantic ANSI coloring (Red for errors, Yellow for warnings, Cyan for processing, Blue for summaries) and progress counters `[i/N]` significantly improve scannability and reduce user anxiety during long-running tasks.
**Action:** Always include a final summary report and progress indicators when building CLI-driven automation scripts to provide clear closure and status feedback.
